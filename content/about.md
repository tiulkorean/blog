**Tiul Korean** is a place to learn real Korean and share what makes it fun — the expressions you hear in K-dramas, K-pop and variety shows, and the everyday phrases Koreans actually use.

Each lesson takes one expression and explains what it really means, with romanization, example sentences, a vocabulary list and a quick quiz.

*Tiul* is a little sprout: one new expression at a time, growing into real Korean.

Every lesson is tagged **Beginner**, **Intermediate** or **Advanced**, so you can browse by [level](/levels/) or by [topic](/categories/).

Some lesson material is gathered from public data and open APIs, then edited and reviewed before publishing. When a lesson is based on an outside source, it is credited at the bottom of the page.
