**정** (*jeong*) is the warm attachment that builds up slowly when you spend time with someone — a friend, a neighbor, or even an old café you keep going back to.

Unlike love at first sight, jeong is never instant. It **accumulates**, which is why Koreans say 정이 들다 — jeong “enters” you, little by little.
