If you’ve watched even one episode of a K-drama, you’ve heard **어떡해** (*eotteokae*). Someone drops a ring into the river, misses the last bus, or gets caught lying — and out it comes, usually twice in a row: *어떡해, 어떡해!*

It comes from **어떻게 해** — literally “how (should I) do it?” In everyday speech the two words squeeze together into one quick, breathless sound.

## How it’s used

어떡해 isn’t really a question you expect an answer to. It’s a **reaction**, like *Oh no!* or *What am I going to do?!* in English.

You’ll also hear it when characters feel bad *for* someone: a friend tells a sad story, and the other person says **어떡해…** softly — closer to *Oh no, that’s awful.*

## Spelling tip

Even Koreans mix this up! **어떡해** (*what do I do*) is correct. ~~어떻해~~ is a very common misspelling you’ll see in comments and texts.
