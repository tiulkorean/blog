Before picking up their chopsticks, Koreans say **잘 먹겠습니다** (*jal meokgetseumnida*) — literally “I will eat well.” It’s a small thank-you to whoever cooked, paid or invited you.

When the meal is over, the phrase flips to the past tense: **잘 먹었습니다** — “I ate well.” You’ll hear people say it to friends, parents, and even to restaurant owners on their way out the door.

With close friends, you can shorten both: **잘 먹을게!** before and **잘 먹었어!** after.
