In Korean, the same sentence changes depending on **who you are talking to**. Speaking politely is called **존댓말** (*jondaenmal*), and casual speech is **반말** (*banmal*).

As a learner, the safest rule is simple: **use 존댓말 with anyone you don’t know well**, and with anyone older than you.

## It’s more than adding 요

Many learners think polite speech is just adding **-요** to the end of a sentence. That’s a big part of it — but some common words change completely when you talk *about* someone you respect. You’ll find the most important ones in the vocabulary list below.

## The moment to drop politeness

Koreans usually start with 존댓말, and once they become closer, one person suggests switching: **말 놓을까요?** or **말 편하게 할까요?** It’s most natural for the older person to offer first.

Switching to 반말 *before* this conversation can come across as rude — even if you are trying to be friendly.
